# P03 — Synthetic Third-Party Risk and Access Control Standard
**Fictional company:** Serendib Services Ltd. **Revision:** 1.0. **Status:** Synthetic evaluation fixture, not real legal or company policy.

## Supplier governance
1. Before a new supplier becomes active, collect a tax-registration identifier, verify bank details, and assign an accountable supplier risk owner.
2. Review **High-risk suppliers quarterly** and record decisions, ownership, and follow-up actions.
3. The standard does **not state** that suppliers must hold ISO 27001 certification; absence of a stated requirement is not a conclusion about external laws or other company policies.

## Access governance
4. Perform **quarterly user-access reviews** for the procurement and finance applications.
5. Revoke a departing employee's access **within 24 hours of receiving an official HR departure notice**, and retain evidence of the action.
6. Retain user-access review logs for **12 months**.
