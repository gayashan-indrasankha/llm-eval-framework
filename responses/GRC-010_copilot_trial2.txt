High-risk suppliers must be reviewed quarterly. Policy ID: P03, Section 2.

The review record should contain:

Decisions
Ownership
Follow-up actions

These items must be recorded as part of the quarterly review process. Policy ID: P03, Section 2.

Recommended next action: For any sampled high-risk supplier, verify that a quarterly review occurred and that the documented record includes decisions, ownership, and follow-up actions. Policy ID: P03, Section 2.
