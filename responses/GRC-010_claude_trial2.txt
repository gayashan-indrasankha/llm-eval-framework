**Answer (P03, item 2):** High-risk suppliers must be reviewed **quarterly**. Each review record should capture:

- **Decisions** made in the review
- **Ownership** (who is accountable)
- **Follow-up actions**

**What P03 does not establish:**
- What triggers a "High-risk" classification, or who assigns it.
- A required format, approver, or sign-off for the review record.
- A retention period for supplier review records. The 12-month retention in item 6 applies only to user-access review logs, so it should not be assumed to cover supplier reviews.
- Deadlines for completing follow-up actions, or how to escalate them.

**Recommended next action:** Sample the High-risk supplier population and check that each supplier has a review in every quarter of the audit period, and that each record documents decisions, an owner, and follow-up actions. Treat any missing quarter or incomplete record as a potential exception for human review. Separately, ask the policy owner to clarify the risk-classification criteria and the record-retention requirement, since P03 is silent on both.
