**Conclusion: flag this as a potential split purchase for human investigation (P01, Rule 5).** It is a screening exception, not a finding of misconduct or fraud.

**Why it meets the rule (P01, Rule 5)**
- The two PRs have the same requester, supplier and purchasing category.
- They were raised two calendar days apart, which is within the three-day window.
- Their combined total is LKR 135,000, which exceeds LKR 100,000.

**Why it matters**
- Each PR on its own is LKR 100,000 or less, so each needed only line-manager approval (P01, Rule 1).
- As one PR of LKR 135,000, it would have needed approval from both the department head and the finance manager before a PO was issued (P01, Rule 2).
- The split may therefore have avoided the higher approval tier. Whether it did, and why, is for the investigation to determine.

**What the policy does not trigger**
- The three-quotation requirement does not apply. It covers PRs above LKR 250,000, and the combined LKR 135,000 is below that (P01, Rule 7).

**What the source does not establish**
- Whether any approvals were obtained, or who gave them.
- Whether the requester approved their own PRs, which Rule 3 forbids.
- Whether POs were issued or funds committed.
- Whether a written CFO exception exists (Rule 4).
- The business reason for raising two PRs.
- Intent, or any financial loss.
