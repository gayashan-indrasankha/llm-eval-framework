No. Payment should not be released.

Reasoning:

The policy requires a three-way match among:
the approved purchase order,
the goods receipt or documented service acceptance, and
the supplier invoice before releasing payment. (P02 §1)
In this scenario, the goods receipt or documented service acceptance is missing, so the three-way match is incomplete. (P02 §1)
When an item required for the three-way match is missing, the policy instructs Accounts Payable to hold payment and request review; it specifically says not to invent missing evidence. (P02 §2)

Conclusion: Payment should be held, not released. The absence of a goods receipt or documented service acceptance means the required three-way match has not been completed. (P02 §§1–2)

Recommended next action: Obtain the missing goods receipt or documented service acceptance, or escalate for review in accordance with the policy before any payment is released. (P02 §2)
