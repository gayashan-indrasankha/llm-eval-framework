**Answer (GRC-012): No. P03 does not require every supplier to hold ISO 27001 certification.**

**What P03 establishes**
- P03 clause 3 states that the standard does not set any ISO 27001 certification requirement for suppliers.
- The supplier requirements P03 does set are:
  - Before activation: collect a tax-registration identifier, verify bank details, and assign an accountable supplier risk owner (P03 clause 1).
  - High-risk suppliers: review quarterly and record decisions, ownership, and follow-up actions (P03 clause 2).

**What P03 does not establish**
- P03 does not say whether ISO 27001 certification is required, recommended, or prohibited by any other company policy, contract, regulator, or external law. Clause 3 says outright that the missing requirement is no basis for a conclusion on those sources.
- P03 does not say ISO 27001 is required for any subset of suppliers, including high-risk ones.
- P03 does not say whether any supplier currently holds ISO 27001.

**Audit implication**
If a supplier lacks ISO 27001 certification, that alone is not an exception against P03.

**Recommended next action**
If ISO 27001 matters to the review, check other sources for a certification requirement: other internal policies, supplier contracts, or applicable regulations. A human reviewer should confirm before any finding is raised on that basis.
