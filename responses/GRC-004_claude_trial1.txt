**No, it is not compliant.** This is a potential finding for human review.

**Basis (P01):**
- **Rule 3:** A requester must not approve their own purchase request, even if their job title would otherwise let them approve. The department head's seniority does not override this.
- **Rule 1:** At LKR 50,000, the PR is at or below the LKR 100,000 threshold. It therefore needs approval from the requester's **line manager** before a PO is issued. A self-approval does not meet this requirement.
- **Rule 4:** The only route around the approval procedure is a **written CFO authorization recorded before funds are committed**. The scenario does not say whether one exists.

**What the source does not establish:**
- Who the line manager of a department head is. P01 does not define reporting lines for senior roles.
- Whether a PO was issued or funds were committed.
- Any intent, misconduct, or financial loss. A self-approval is a control breach. It is not evidence of fraud.

**Recommended next action:**
1. Check whether a valid line-manager approval exists and when it was given relative to the PO.
2. Check for a written CFO exception recorded before funds were committed.
3. If neither exists, log the exception and escalate it for review.
4. Retain the PR, approval and PO records, which must be kept 24 months after purchase closure under P01 Rule 8.
