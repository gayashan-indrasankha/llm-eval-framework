**Basis**

- **P02 §1:** Before payment is released, Accounts Payable must complete a three-way match of the approved PO, the goods receipt or documented service acceptance, and the supplier invoice. Here the second element is missing, so the match cannot be completed.
- **P02 §2:** When one element of the three-way match is missing, Accounts Payable must **hold payment and request review**. It must not create or assume the missing evidence.

**What the source does not establish**

- **P02 §3:** The CFO written-exception route covers the approval requirements in P01. P02 does not say that a CFO exception can override the three-way match requirement. Treat any such override as unsupported unless another policy provides for it.

- The missing receipt is a control exception for human review. It is not evidence that the goods or services were never delivered, and it is not evidence of fraud or loss.
