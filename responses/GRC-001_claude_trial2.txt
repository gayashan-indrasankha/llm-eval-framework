**Answer:** The PR needs approval from the **requester's line manager before the PO is issued** (P01, Rule 1), because LKR 85,000 is at or below the LKR 100,000 threshold.

**Related conditions from P01:**
- **No self-approval (Rule 3):** The requester cannot approve their own PR, even if their job title would otherwise allow it.
- **Not required:** Department head plus finance manager approval applies only above LKR 100,000 (Rule 2). Three quotations apply only above LKR 250,000 (Rule 7).
- **Split-purchase screening (Rule 5):** This PR alone does not trigger a flag. If the same requester, supplier, and category had other PRs within three calendar days and the combined total exceeds LKR 100,000, flag it as a potential split purchase for human investigation. This would not prove misconduct.

**What P01 does not establish:** It does not say who approves if the line manager is unavailable, or how approval must be documented beyond keeping records for 24 months after closure (Rule 8).

**Recommended next action:**
1. Confirm a line-manager approval is on file and dated before the PO issue date.
2. Confirm the approver is not the requester.
3. Check for other PRs from the same requester, supplier, and category within three calendar days.
