**Control gap (P01, Rule 2):** A PR of LKR 145,000 is above the LKR 100,000 threshold, so it needs approval from **both the department head and the finance manager** before a PO is issued. Line manager approval meets only the requirement for PRs of LKR 100,000 or less (P01, Rule 1), so it is not sufficient here. This is a **potential finding requiring human review**. It is not evidence of fraud or financial loss.

**What the source does not establish:**
- Whether a PO was actually issued or funds committed. The gap is a breach only if the PO went out before the required approvals (P01, Rule 2).
- Whether the line manager also holds the department head role. Even then, finance manager approval would still be missing.
- Whether a written CFO authorization for an exception was recorded before funds were committed (P01, Rule 4). An informal email from the requester would not count.
- Whether the requester approved their own PR (P01, Rule 3). Nothing indicates this.
- Whether related PRs exist that would trigger the split-purchase screen (P01, Rules 5–6).

**Not applicable:** Three competitive quotations are not required, because LKR 145,000 does not exceed LKR 250,000 (P01, Rule 7).

**Recommended next action:**
1. Check the PR and PO records to see whether a PO was issued and when.
2. Look for department head and finance manager approvals, or a pre-dated written CFO exception (P01, Rules 2 and 4).
3. Screen for other PRs from the same requester, supplier and category within three calendar days (P01, Rule 5).
4. Retain all related records (P01, Rule 8).
5. Escalate to the responsible reviewer for disposition.
