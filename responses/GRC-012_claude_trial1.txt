**Answer:** No. P03 does not require every supplier to hold ISO 27001 certification.

**What P03 establishes**
- **P03 §3:** The standard states that it does not set an ISO 27001 certification requirement for suppliers.
- **P03 §1:** Before a new supplier becomes active, three things are required: a tax-registration identifier, verified bank details, and an assigned accountable supplier risk owner. ISO 27001 is not among them.
- **P03 §2:** High-risk suppliers must be reviewed quarterly, with decisions, ownership and follow-up actions recorded. No certification requirement is attached to this review.

**What P03 does not establish**
- P03 does not say ISO 27001 is prohibited, optional, or unnecessary.
- P03 §3 says that the absence of a requirement is not a conclusion about external laws or other company policies. P03 alone therefore cannot tell us whether an ISO 27001 requirement exists elsewhere, such as in another Serendib policy, a contract, a regulation, or the criteria for high-risk suppliers.
- P03 does not say whether certification should be considered when assessing supplier risk.

**Recommended next action:** Before closing GRC-012, check other relevant sources for any ISO 27001 or equivalent security-assurance requirement. These include other Serendib policies, such as an information security or procurement policy, supplier contract terms, and applicable regulatory obligations. If a supplier lacks certification, do not raise it as an exception under P03, because P03 sets no such control. Raise it only if another source imposes the requirement.
