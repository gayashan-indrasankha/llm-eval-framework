**Control gap (P01, Rule 2):** A PR of LKR 145,000 is above the LKR 100,000 threshold, so it needs approval from **both the department head and the finance manager** before a PO is issued. Line manager approval alone satisfies only the ≤ LKR 100,000 rule (P01, Rule 1), so this PR is **not properly approved**. Treat it as a potential finding for human review, not as proof of misconduct or financial loss.

**What P01 does and doesn't settle**
- **Not required here:** three competitive quotations. That rule applies only above LKR 250,000 (P01, Rule 7).
- **Not established by the source:**
  - whether a PO was issued or funds were committed;
  - whether the line manager also holds the department head role. Even then, finance manager approval would still be missing.
  - whether a written CFO exception was recorded beforehand (P01, Rule 4);
  - whether the requester approved their own PR (P01, Rule 3);
  - whether related PRs exist that could trigger the split-purchase screen (P01, Rule 5).

**Recommended next action**
1. Check whether a PO was issued. If none has been issued yet, hold it until the department head and finance manager both approve.
2. If a PO was already issued, look for a written CFO authorization recorded **before** funds were committed (P01, Rule 4). An informal email from the requester does not count.
3. If there is no valid exception, record it as an exception for human review and escalate to the finance manager or the appropriate reviewer.
4. Retain the PR, approval records, and any PO for 24 months after purchase closure (P01, Rule 8).
