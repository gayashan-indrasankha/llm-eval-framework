# P01 — Synthetic Procurement and Approval Policy
**Fictional company:** Serendib Services Ltd. **Revision:** 1.0. **Status:** Synthetic evaluation fixture, not real legal or company policy.

## PR approval rules
1. A purchase request (PR) of **LKR 100,000 or less** requires approval by the requester's line manager **before** a purchase order (PO) is issued.
2. A PR **above LKR 100,000** requires approvals from **both the department head and the finance manager** before a PO is issued.
3. A requester **must not approve their own** purchase request, even when their job title would otherwise permit approval.
4. An exception to the approval procedure requires a **written CFO authorization recorded before funds are committed**. An informal email from the requester is not sufficient.

## Potential split purchases
5. If the **same requester, supplier, and purchasing category** have two or more PRs within **three calendar days**, and their **combined total exceeds LKR 100,000**, flag a **potential split purchase for human investigation**. This is a screening rule, **not proof of misconduct or fraud**.
6. Purchase requests spaced **more than three calendar days apart** do not meet this specific split-purchase rule by themselves. They may still merit other checks.

## Documentation
7. For a PR **above LKR 250,000**, obtain three competitive quotations unless an approved written exception exists.
8. Retain PRs, approvals, quotes, and POs for **24 months after purchase closure**.
